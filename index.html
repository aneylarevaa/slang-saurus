Slang Saurus 🦖

Kamus slang bahasa Inggris lengkap dengan arti, konteks, dan contoh kalimatnya.

TBH (To Be Honest) Opinions
Arti: Sejujurnya / Sebenarnya
"TBH, the horror movie we watched yesterday wasn't that scary."
Rizz Gen Z / Internet
Arti: Kemampuan karisma atau merayu seseorang
"Bro walked up and got her number instantly, his rizz is unmatched."
ASAP Corporate / Texting
Arti: Secepat mungkin (As Soon As Possible)
"Please send the report files over to me ASAP."
Slay Gen Z / Pop Culture
Arti: Tampil luar biasa, keren banget, atau memukau
"You look incredible in that outfit, absolutely slay!"
EOD (End of Day) Corporate
Arti: Batas akhir waktu kerja hari ini
"Please submit your activity reports before EOD."
